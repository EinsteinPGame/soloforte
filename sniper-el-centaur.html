EL CENTAUR

Rooftop Contract — Playable Core

You are a master sniper of the El Centaur, a machine species at war. Armour keeps you alive; the gravity grapple keeps you invisible.

The loop: shoot from a rooftop → they begin working out where you are → grapple away before they finish → fire from a new angle. Every shot is a choice: one more kill from here, or move first.

Three shots from the same roof and you are located. Snipers aim, and the spotter calls artillery on your last known position — so make sure that is not where you are standing.
